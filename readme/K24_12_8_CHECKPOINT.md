# K24.12.8 — EOD PDF Drink Summary — Cups format

Updated the EOD PDF Drink Summary — Cups to use the requested fixed cup layout:

Drink | Hot 12oz | Iced 12oz | Iced 22oz | Iced 1L | Total

Hot Coffee is always shown as its own row. Hot Coffee quantities are counted in the Hot 12oz column, with zeroes in the iced columns. Other drink products are counted in the iced size columns.

This change applies to the EOD PDF report only. Existing meal and accessory summaries are unchanged.
